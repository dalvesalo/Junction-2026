Finland · National Cancer Strategy Goal 3 · equal and timely treatment

Two patients. Same lymphoma. Seven weeks apart.

In Finland the first door you knock on decides how fast you are diagnosed. Patients who enter through public primary care reach treatment months after those who enter through occupational or private care — and almost all of that time is lost before the specialist ever sees them.

?

How long does a Finnish patient wait between a first symptom and a cancer diagnosis? No routine statistic answers that. The strategy says the data "is not collected systematically", and cancer target times are not published by county — while Denmark publishes them by region every quarter. You cannot shorten a wait you do not see.

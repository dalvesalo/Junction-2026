# Corrections after independent fact-check (QA_REPORT.md) — apply everywhere

1. **Kuitunen et al., Cancer Med 2026;15(5):e71920** — multi-centre: 4 university + 3 secondary hospitals, n = 160, prospective 2014–2020. NOT "North Ostrobothnia".
   - Tested headline: median symptom-to-treatment **127 days (public primary care by appointment) vs 76 days (private/occupational)**, p = 0.027 → **51 days ≈ 7 weeks** later. Means 201 vs 126.
   - Step chart 42/43/77/118/123 vs 72/94/147/204/207 = **cumulative step times from the paper's Fig. 1** (provider labels as shown in the AstraZeneca keynote; stat type not stated, values close to the means). Use only with that label.
   - New hook: **"Two patients. Same lymphoma. Seven weeks apart."** Do not say "twice as long" citing Kuitunen (the roadmap's "twice" refers to an unpublished study — cite the roadmap if used).
2. **Yle MOT** lung-delay article is dated **12.10.2026** (not 2025). Still unpublished study → weak.
3. **Legal:** HE 159/2025 **approved by Parliament 21.4.2026 (EV 38/2026)**; entry-into-force date not verified. Say "approved by Parliament in April 2026", not "legal since 2026".
4. **CT access:** "< 10 % of units" comes from an **expert survey** (Khalife 2025, 42 clinicians, 12 Finnish; summarised by IHE 2026) — say "according to a Nordic expert survey", grade **weak–moderate**. Khalife DOI 10.2340/1651-226X.2025.42778.
5. **Murphy JCO 2015:** trial tracked **abnormal test results without follow-up** (EHR triggers + reviewer). 200 → 104 days is **colorectal**; prostate 192 → 144; **lung arm not significant (n = 19)**. Always say "colorectal".
6. Education survival gap (64 vs 77 %) and the front-door gap are **two separate findings**; no source links entry route to survival. Don't say "people at the public door have 13-point lower survival".
7. Footer: "Research prototype — not CE-marked" (not "Not a medical device").
8. Stage missing: use **61 % (FCR, 2019–2023)** consistently.
9. Roadmap 3.5.3 €1,140,000 for digital/AI early-detection tools — verified in the roadmap PDF (p. 73–74).
10. Engine fixes: negation scope stops at commas; words must match at word start ("retired" ≠ tired, "kitchen" ≠ itch); missing smoking details/age no longer give NaN; explicit referral rules can no longer be overridden by a low risk estimate; "negat" removed as negator.

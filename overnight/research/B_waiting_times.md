# B: Waiting times and access, Finland (cancer pathway relevant)

Research agent B, overnight 13–14.11.2026. Every value below was read from the source named. Where a value came through a press release, news article or secondary report, that is stated. No values were invented. "Fetched via summariser" means the PDF or page was read through WebFetch's summarising model; spot-check the key numbers in the original before putting them on a slide.

Evidence grades: **Strong** = official statistics or registry, with a clear definition. **Moderate** = official but secondary, partial or old, or a peer-reviewed survey. **Weak** = news reporting, an unpublished study, or expert opinion.

Outcome measured: WT = waiting time / access. ST = stage. SV = survival. SR = self-reported access.

## (c) Cancer-specific waiting times

### National targets that already exist
- **THL Ohjaus 4/2017 (earlier 21/2016)**, *Syövän hoitoon pääsyn seuranta – määritelmät ja ohjeet* (https://core.ac.uk/download/145056318.pdf). It sets these targets:
  - suspected cancer, referral to first treatment: **≤6 weeks**
  - urgent cancers: within days, or at most 2–3 weeks from diagnosis
  - imaging report ≤1 week
  - biopsy ≤1 week from request
  - pathology report ≤1 week
  - adjuvant therapy ≤4 weeks after surgery

  It covers 16 cancer types (about 75% of cases). Grade: Strong (official definition). **No published results against these targets were found.** The roadmap says Goal 3 uses "treatment within target times" as an indicator (see LOG, 01:35).
- Järvelin et al., *Lääkärilehti* 45/2019 (https://core.ac.uk/download/270319454.pdf): national monitoring is aggregated only by hospital district and specialty, and its data quality is hard to verify. Only 92% of registry cancer diagnoses appear in Hilmo, and only 44% for brain/CNS cancers. Grade: Moderate. This shows the current data cannot support cancer waiting-time monitoring.

**Pitch framing:** The other Nordic countries also miss their own pathway targets, yet they **measure** them publicly, by region and by cancer, every quarter. Finland has defined targets (THL 4/2017) but publishes no results. The gap to show is a measurement gap plus a front-door (primary care / CT) gap. The hospital queue itself is not where the gap is.

## (e) Data gaps
1. **Current (2025–2026) per-county primary care access** (THL monthly) was not retrievable. thl.fi, sampo.thl.fi, sotkanet.fi and julkari.fi are blocked for this session (both WebFetch and the shell proxy). The latest county table I could get is March 2024.
2. **No published cancer waiting-time results** against the THL 4/2017 targets, by county or by cancer type. Yle MOT (10/2026) confirms that Finland has no comprehensive national data on cancer delays.
3. **The symptom → GP → referral interval is not measured** in any official statistic. The only figures are the unpublished Kuittinen lung study (41 d primary care) and the IHE/Khalife survey (14 d to a health-centre appointment).
4. Specialised-care queue data is by specialty, not by cancer, and covers only patients already in the queue. Oncology medians (HUS 7–8 d) hide the pre-referral delay.
5. Occupational health care coverage by county is not published in an accessible form.
6. THL register data excludes outsourced or purchased services (THL project final report, 2026), so county comparisons are biased.
7. Per-county >180-day figures for 2026: only regulatory lists are available, with no numbers.

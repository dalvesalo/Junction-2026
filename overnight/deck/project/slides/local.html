Where a county can act

The variation is local — at the health-centre level

67 %

of the variation in cancer survival between Finnish municipalities lies inside hospital districts, not between them.

Survival differences between counties are mostly not statistically significant. The differences that matter sit closer to the patient: in which health centre, which door, which week.

So the fix belongs to the county — per health centre — not to a national reform that is years away.

Seppä et al., Acta Oncol 2020 — strong · Finnish Cancer Registry, Cancer in Finland 2023; OECD 2025

APPENDIX / Q&A. Why we target health centres not counties. Honest point: county survival gaps are small; within-district variation dominates.

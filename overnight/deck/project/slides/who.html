Who pays for the slow door

It lands on those who can’t skip the queue

64 % vs 77 %

5-year cancer survival, men with basic vs higher education (women: 65 % vs 78 %).

  • Less-educated patients: more stage IV, less surgery, 13–14 % higher cancer-specific mortality in a national register study.
  • Occupational care covers 92 % of wage earners. Retired, unemployed and low-income people use the public door.

OECD/EC Country Cancer Profile Finland 2025 (FCR data) · Zai et al., BMJ Public Health 2025 (n = 469,522) · strong · Kela 2026

STAGE (11 s). Who uses the public door? Occupational care covers 92 % of wage earners — so it's the retired, the unemployed, the low-paid. And less-educated patients' cancer survival is thirteen points lower.

Ajoissa: a safety net for public primary care

Read

Overnight: visit notes (FI/EN), labs, visit history — the county’s own records

Weigh

Bayesian risk: smoking-history prior × published symptom likelihood ratios

Match

Explicit referral rules (3 % threshold) — the criteria roadmap 2.5.2 will standardise

Act

A named nurse’s worklist: one proposed action, a reason, a source

Track

A pathway clock until diagnosis or all-clear. Overdue? Back on top.

Deterministic: same record, same answer. A professional decides every action. Nothing is sent to a patient automatically.

STAGE (20 s). So we built Ajoissa — ‘in time’. Every night it reads the health centre’s own records, weighs the cancer signals with published evidence, and puts the patients whose signals have stalled on a named nurse’s list, with the reason and the source. Then it runs a clock until each one is diagnosed or cleared. A human decides every step.

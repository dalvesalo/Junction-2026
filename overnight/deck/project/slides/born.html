Where the gap emerges

The delay is born before the specialist

Public primary-care route drawn to scale, in days from the first symptom. Red: extra days compared with occupational or private care at each step (cumulative step times, Fig. 1). After the specialist visit the gap stops growing, so the delay comes from the public front door. The national roadmap describes the same pattern from another, unpublished lymphoma study.

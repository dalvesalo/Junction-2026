STAGE (24 s). Two Finns notice the same lump. One has occupational health care, one goes to the public health centre. Same lymphoma: 76 days to treatment through occupational or private care — 127 through public primary care. Seven weeks. And the gap is already there by the specialist visit; after that, both routes take three to five days. The delay is born at the front door.

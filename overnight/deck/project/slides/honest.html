Data quality and transparency

What is real, what is modelled, what is invented

Real

Nordic survival, education gap, DLBCL route timing, county access (THL, March 2024), legal changes — each with source and grade.

Modelled

Impact numbers. Risk estimates use UK likelihood ratios and PLCOm2012 — both need recalibration on Finnish data.

Synthetic

All patients, health centres and timestamps in the demo. Tagged on every screen.

APPENDIX / Q&A. Limitations: DLBCL values read from a slide; county access data is pre-2025; 41-day lung interval unpublished.
